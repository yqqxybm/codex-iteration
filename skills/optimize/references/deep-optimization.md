# Deep Optimization

Deep optimization means deeper contact with the object, not a longer ritual.
Use it when ordinary local correction could miss the relations, history,
alternative futures, or practical consequences that govern the work.

## Deepen The Judgment

Begin by asking whether the inherited definition of the object conceals a
decisive possibility. Neither an existing form nor an inherited diagnosis fixes
the space of possible improvement. Deep inquiry may reconstruct that form;
it must not silently replace the user's purpose or adopt a new product
commitment. Use `project-lifecycle` to select the unresolved project owner, or
`three-step-analysis` for a consequential non-project judgment.

Expand the inquiry far enough to understand:

- the object and its purpose in the concrete situation;
- the relations, dependencies, history, affected parties, and pressures that
  may govern success or failure;
- the leading contradiction or unrealized possibility, rather than a list of
  disconnected defects;
- live alternatives, including preservation, reversal, and a materially
  different reconstruction;
- the consequences the proposed change would create after the local defect is
  gone.

Select lenses because of unanswered questions. `three-step-analysis` helps when
the practical direction is genuinely unsettled; `self-refine` helps where the
direction is accepted and the expression is weak; a domain owner helps where
its knowledge defines the object. Each lens earns its place by changing or
testing the emerging judgment.

## Change And Return

Make the change that the judgment requires within authorization. Do not assume
that minimal change is virtuous: preserve what carries the purpose, but
reconstruct a mechanism when its form is the problem.

Then return from the change to the whole. Ask whether it has actually improved
the object's capacity to realize its purpose, what it has displaced or made
harder, and whether a relation previously treated as peripheral is now decisive.
Test the strongest relevant alternative or counterexample when it can overturn
the choice. Revise or undo the change when its consequences falsify the
judgment.

## Review And Closeout

For review-optimize requests, let an initial review diagnose the object; use
its findings as possible inputs to the optimization judgment. Review remains
read-only, and not every finding deserves a change.

Close with renewed review when the change affects a key claim, a governing
relation, safety, continuity, or the concern that initiated the work. Otherwise
the proportional verification chosen by the owner is enough. Further inquiry
is justified by a material change in the judgment, the reality, or the user's
accepted purpose.

## Stop

Stop when a whole-object rereading finds no credible, authorized change that
would more fully realize the purpose after considering its consequences and
preservation boundary. State any unresolved uncertainty and the condition that
would justify reopening the work. Deep work is complete when its practical
judgment is adequate to the concrete situation and remains corrigible.
