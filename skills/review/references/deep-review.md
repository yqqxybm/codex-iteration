# Deep Review

Load this reference for explicit deep, exhaustive, 全面, 穷尽, 逐词逐句, or
repeated review. It intensifies the same independent, read-only judgment by
following the object and its consequential relations further.

## What Depth Means

Depth first tests whether the current definition of the object faithfully
contains the reality relevant to its purpose, or hides a possibility that would
change the whole judgment. When the definition itself fails, report that
upstream limitation rather than treating its inherited boundaries as fact.

Deep review follows the object more completely through the relations that make
its purpose possible or impossible. It tests the leading account against
counterexamples, adjacent conditions, failure paths, historical assumptions,
and likely consequences. It may move back and forth between the whole and a
detail whenever the detail changes the whole judgment.

Choose the sequence, perspectives, and amount of reconsideration from the
object itself. Stop when the material relationships have been pursued far
enough to support a proportionate judgment, or when a real boundary makes
further inquiry unable to change it.

## Exhaustive Requests

For an explicit exhaustive request, traverse every materially relevant
direction within the agreed target, especially directions where error or harm
would be consequential. The extent of traversal follows risk, object, and user
intent. Preserve a focused object's boundary unless the judgment itself shows
that the wider whole is materially implicated.

## Reality Contact

Inspect the reality most capable of changing the judgment: direct behavior,
contradicting cases, affected experience, operational conditions, tests, or
comparisons as the object requires.

When a needed verification cannot be performed, ask whether its absence limits
the conclusion. Mention it only if it does. A deep review can still reach a
bounded judgment without pretending that uncertainty has disappeared.

## Result

Return the core judgment, material findings or reasons for adequacy, and the
practical implication. Preserve findings-first and severity for code review.
State scope, depth, or unexamined material directions only when they qualify
the conclusion. Review returns judgment to its caller; action and lifecycle
continuity remain with their respective owners.
