# Cyclic Review And Improvement

Use when the user explicitly asks for cyclic/repeated review or optimization,
including `目标！循环深度审查优化`. This governs convergence, not the content of
the inquiry. Reading it does not create a native goal without an explicit goal
request. Ordinary deep work retains its own proportionate stopping judgment.

## Establish What Better Means

Use the goal's object, purpose, quality judgment, and authorized scope. Derive
the consequential questions and perspectives before treating the current
finding list as the work. Review asks what is adequate or limiting; optimization
also develops and compares worthwhile unrealized possibilities. Neither the
current design nor an empty defect list fixes what could be better.

An improvement needs a credible account of how it serves the purpose after
considering its consequences, costs, and what must be preserved. A new idea
need not have a preexisting example, but novelty alone is insufficient. Do not
exclude a worthwhile in-scope improvement merely by calling it optional polish.
Changes to the user's purpose or commitments require the appropriate dialogue
and owner; broader inquiry does not itself widen mutation authority.

## Iterate On The Actual Object

Use the user's requested count and scope. When a cyclic deep review/improvement
request leaves the count unspecified, use two consecutive clean whole-scope
passes as the default stopping check. Choose stronger work when the actual
stakes or explicit claim require it, and explain why. Do not silently lower
the agreed bar when completion becomes difficult.

For each pass, use `review` for independent judgment of the current object and
its consequential relations. When improvement is authorized, use `optimize`
to consider meaningful alternatives, not merely repair the review's findings;
let its selected inquiry and domain owners do the work. Select cognitive
frameworks for unresolved questions rather than rotate through their names.

A clean pass has considered the relevant whole and strongest live alternatives
or countercases and found no substantive unresolved limitation or credible,
authorized improvement worth making. For review-only work it means no new
material findings, not permission to repair or proof that known findings are
resolved. Keep any known findings visible in the final review judgment.

After an accepted material change, verify its consequences and reset the clean
count to zero. Next review the changed whole, not just the repaired diff.
Two parallel reviews of the same state are inputs to one pass, not two sequential
passes. The next pass must independently reconsider the latest whole; reading
the prior findings or repeating the same completion claim is not another pass.
Keep only the pass status and decisive judgment needed in the existing task
state, not a new proof ledger. Report only passes that actually occurred.

## Conclude Honestly

Stop when the requested result and delivery are complete, the consecutive pass
condition holds after the last material change, and no required work remains.
For review-only work, delivering the adequate assessment and its findings can
fulfill the commission without changing the reviewed object.

A blocked necessary check or unresolved decision is not a clean pass. A later
invocation reconsiders present reality and the basis of the prior judgment; it
need not manufacture a new change. Reopen for a consequential new finding,
possibility, condition, or user purpose, not for novelty or another invocation
alone. Convergence means a sufficiently tested practical judgment in the current
scope and conditions, not proof that no better arrangement can ever exist.
